Yardage — Support

Yardage is a digital yardage book for iPhone and Apple Watch. Plan a hole before you play it, log the round in a couple of taps, and read it back like a caddie's notebook. Stroke play, match play, Best Ball and Four-Ball, with GPS distances, conditions-adjusted club suggestions, and statistics written as observations rather than a dashboard.

Common questions

- No distances? Turn on GPS under Settings → GPS & Live Activity, allow Location for Yardage, and make sure the hole has a tee and green marked in Course Map.
- Distance looks wrong? Move the green pin in Course Map. The fix applies to every round at that course.
- Watch not updating? Open the Yardage app on the Watch. Edits queue and send once the phone is reachable.
- Handicap shows "—"? It needs three completed rounds, or set a starting index in Settings. Nine-hole rounds pair with another nine at the same course before counting.
- Round missing from Apple Fitness? Health needs permission, and Save Golf Workouts must have been on when the round started. Open the round to retry.

Your rounds stay on your device — no account, no cloud sync. Back up from Settings → Backup & Restore. The handicap figure is a local estimate, not an official Handicap Index.
